Serkiler — Lembar Edaran (Curated Roster)

Physical circulation sheet for a curated subset of members (~22 of 30 here, e.g. perangkat + sesepuh). Print blank sheet → bagikan untuk paraf → record kembali di sini. Roster managed via "+ Tambah Jama'ah ke Serkiler".
