Jama'ah — Daftar Jama'ah & KK

Member directory organized by household (KK). Each household is the primary visual unit — families cluster together, with members nested beneath a header that names the kepala keluarga. Toggle to Daftar for a flat sortable table when you need to scan across households. Same visual system as Absensi: cream paper, Plex Sans/Mono, 38px rows.

↓
Try toggling Per KK ↔ Daftar, click a household header to collapse it, change Kelas, toggle Aktif saja off to reveal inactive members (mutasi). Drag a KK by its grip handle to reorder — numbers auto-renumber.
